Expanded Senses Cane
Milwaukee School of Engineering
Aug 2018 – May 2019

Assistive device improving obstacle detection for the visually impaired. Programmed sensors and a camera on Raspberry Pi using Python and OpenCV; built the electronics from scratch, fabricated custom PCBs in Eagle CAD, and 3D-modeled enclosures in Autodesk Inventor. Produced two prototypes — an MVP 6-ft pole and a final cane. Won $1,500. Team of 5 from diverse backgrounds.
