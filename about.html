Who I Am

I'm Shanthosh Reddy Gollapalli Raghunatha — an engineer, product thinker, and builder based in Farmington, Michigan. I hold an M.S. in Management Information Systems from the University of Illinois Springfield (Beta Gamma Sigma, Chicoine Scholar) and a B.S. in Computer Engineering from the Milwaukee School of Engineering.

At Visteon Corporation, I work as a Senior Engineer on next-generation cockpit electronics — applying AI/ML models and algorithms to develop In-Vehicle AI Assistants, ADAS, Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS), and Surround View Monitor (SVM) systems on Android Automotive platforms.

My career has taken me from building serverless cloud functions and Stripe payment integrations at a startup, to managing enterprise IT operations at a municipal utility district, to professional software engineering and product strategy at scale. I thrive where deep technical work meets real business outcomes.

Key interests: Artificial Intelligence · Automotive Technology · Data Science · Software Development
